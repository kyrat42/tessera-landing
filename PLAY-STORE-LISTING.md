## Full description (4000 char max)

```
Tired of juggling five apps just to remember your day? Tessera brings your tasks, routines, and to-do lists into one mindful daily planner — so nothing important gets lost between a dozen different tools.

THE MOSAIC CONCEPT
Your life isn't one long list — it's made of distinct areas: work, health, family, and more. Tessera turns each one into its own color-coded tile, so you always know where your time and attention are going.

BUILT FOR REAL LIFE, NOT PERFECT DAYS
• Weekly Template — set a flexible rhythm for your week once, and Tessera fills it in automatically every day, without locking you into a rigid, minute-by-minute schedule.
• Priority Levels — rank tasks within each area so you always know what matters most right now (choose from Basic, Eisenhower Matrix, ABC Method, or Now/Next/Later).
• Weekly Hours by Area — see exactly how many hours each part of your life is actually getting, at a glance.
• Reminders — never forget a task with nudges timed to when they matter.
• Forgiving by design — unfinished tasks quietly resurface the next day. A rough day never turns into a lost task.

WHY BETA TESTERS SWITCHED TO TESSERA
"Helped me keep track and prioritize my tasks better, as well as calculate how much time I'm spending in each area." — Tessera Beta Tester

Whether you're balancing a 9-to-5 with family time, a flexible freelance schedule, or a household full of moving parts, Tessera helps you plan a week that actually fits your life — not someone else's idea of a perfect one.

Tessera is currently in open beta and completely free to use while testing. Join now and help shape the app as it grows.
```
